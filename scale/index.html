How to measure

  1. Fill in the patient's age, height and sex. The machine needs them to work out body fat.
  2. Turn on Bluetooth (and Location on Android). Tap Connect machine and choose your machine.
  3. Stand on the machine barefoot, on the metal pads, and stay still until the reading appears.
  4. Tap Print report / PDF.
