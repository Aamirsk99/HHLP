How to measure

  1. Close the MovingLife app if it is open: the machine talks to one app at a time.
  2. Choose the profile and check its age, height and sex (Profiles tab). The machine needs them for body fat.
  3. Turn on Bluetooth (and Location on Android), then search and bind the machine.
  4. Stand on it barefoot, on the metal pads, and stay still until the reading appears.
