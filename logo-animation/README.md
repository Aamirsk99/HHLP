# Hindivine Healthcare — Animated Logo Reveal

Intro animation of the Hindivine logo, rebuilt as vector shapes so every
part of the figure and every letter moves on its own and stays sharp at any
size.

Animation:

1. Rings ripple out from the centre and a soft blue glow appears.
2. The pieces of the figure fly in from all sides, spinning, and lock together.
3. The head drops in and bounces; a ripple and a pulse go out when it lands.
4. The finished figure glides left into its place.
5. The letters of HINDIVINE spring up one by one.
6. "HEALTHCARE PRIVATE LIMITED" sweeps in, letter by letter.
7. WWW.HINDIVINE.COM fades in, a light shine sweeps across the logo and
   sparkles twinkle, with a slow zoom while it holds.
8. Fades to white in the last 0.5 s, ready to cut into your video.
