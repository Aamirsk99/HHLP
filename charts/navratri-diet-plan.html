  • One proper meal a day — Lunch at 1:30 PM. No dinner.
  • Breakfast is light; skip it if not hungry. After 4:30 PM only water / green tea.
  • No protein shake. Paneer & curd in lunch give the protein.
  • Drink 2.5 – 3 litres of water daily.
  • Use only sendha namak. No onion, garlic, wheat, rice, besan, dal or pulses.
  • Cook in little ghee / peanut oil — roast, tawa or air-fry; avoid fried pakode & puri.
  • Diabetic, pregnant or on medication? Consult your doctor before fasting.
